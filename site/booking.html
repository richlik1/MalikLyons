Short bio

Vintage Que is a Detroit-born hip-hop and R&B artist bringing together melodic hooks, personal storytelling and unapologetic confidence. Writing, hosting and performing since the age of seven, she is the voice behind “Go Up”, “Dear Diary” and “Vintage Chanel”.
