About

Born and raised in Detroit

Vintage Que is a Detroit-born hip-hop and R&B artist whose music brings together melodic hooks, personal storytelling and unapologetic confidence. Her sound moves between street energy and feminine expression, built on catchy, universal choruses.

She has been writing, hosting and performing since the age of seven. Her releases include “Go Up”, “My Man”, “Love Is” featuring Brayden, “Dear Diary” and “Vintage Chanel”. Through Vibin With Vintage Entertainment, she is building an independent artist identity rooted in music, style and connection with her audience.
