Privacy Policy

My Library · Last updated: October 11, 2026

The short version

My Library does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your books and notes

Books you import (PDF, EPUB, DOCX, TXT), the text extracted from them, your reading position, bookmarks, highlights, notes and settings are stored only on your device, inside the app. They are never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them.

Narration

Books are read aloud by the voices built into your device (Apple’s on-device speech). The text is processed on your device. In the web version, some browsers offer “online” voices provided by the browser maker; these are labelled in the voice list and only used if you choose one.

Backups

If you export a backup, a file is created on your device and you choose where it goes (for example Files or iCloud Drive). My Library never sends it anywhere by itself.

Children

My Library collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Contact the developer through the support link on the App Store page.
